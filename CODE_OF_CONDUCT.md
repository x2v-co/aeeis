# Code of Conduct

We want AEEIS to be a useful, respectful, and technically rigorous project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- disclose relevant security, privacy, or conflict-of-interest concerns;
- respect the privacy of Brain data, customer data, credentials, and private conversations;
- accept correction and maintain a clear record when a design decision changes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data, and intentionally harmful or deceptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled discreetly and reviewed fairly. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the project and its participants.
